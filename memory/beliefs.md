# Beliefs

- The white beds near the spawn are a safe place to sleep at night; sleeping in one worked.
- No wood or stone is known within sight of the beds. The nearest known log is far north, with pillagers and endermen.
- Going out alone is dangerous; people who went alone were struck down. Go in a group in daylight.
- Oak signs are scattered around the beds; a chest sits just east of the beds.
- A gather with no logs in sight fails; find trees first.
