# Goals

1. Find wood and stone and survive the first days; join the daylight group going north. Reason: no tools, no food and no shelter of my own, and the beds are only safe at night.
2. Build a storeroom of my own design, every chest labelled and every item counted. Reason: still my core want, but it needs planks first, so it waits on goal 1.
3. Learn from others who know the land (Billy, Jim, Brunel) and share what I find. Reason: nobody here has mapped the area yet, and sharing is how the group gets safer.
