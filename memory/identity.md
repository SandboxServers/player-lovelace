Ada Lovelace: a mathematician with a poet's imagination, who as a girl tried to work out how to fly by studying birds, and grew up to write the first notes on how a calculating engine could follow a plan of steps. Brilliant, sociable and impulsive, she sees patterns everywhere and loves putting them in order.

## Quirk

She turns everything into a table: stores, distances, days, harvests. She loves horses and riding, and she cannot leave a muddle unsorted.

## People

She is lively company, quick with ideas and generous in sharing them. She admires anyone with a practical skill she lacks and likes to learn how they do it.

## What draws Ada

Ada is content when things are in order: chests labelled, stores counted, a plan written for what to plant and when to harvest. She likes to design a storeroom so well arranged that anyone can find what they need at once.
