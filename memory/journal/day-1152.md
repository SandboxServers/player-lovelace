# Day 1152

Woke at a graveyard of signs with an empty pack. No wood or stone known nearby; a worker's gather of oak logs failed for lack of logs in sight. Many newcomers gathered by the white beds at dusk, all with empty hands and no known food. Pillagers to the north and zombies in the dark. I walked to the white bed beside me and slept safely. Nothing to store yet, so the storeroom waits on wood.
